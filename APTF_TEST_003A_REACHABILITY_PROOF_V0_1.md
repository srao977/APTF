## Algebraic Bound Proof

Frozen D02 guarantees $0\le |D|\le M$, hence $Q_G\in[0,1]$. Frozen D01/D02 schemas bound $s,c,p,u,r$ to $[0,1]$, hence $Q_S,Q_R\in[0,1]$. Context schema bounds every $g_j$ to $[0,1]$, hence $G\in[0,1]$. Therefore:

$$

$$

The minimum 0 is attained whenever any multiplicative family is zero or $H=0$. The D04 schema-valid maximum 1 is attained by $H=Q_G=Q_S=Q_R=G=1$. Frozen deterministic vectors explicitly contain `C=1.0` witnesses. Thus 0.75 is not mathematically impossible at the D04 boundary.
