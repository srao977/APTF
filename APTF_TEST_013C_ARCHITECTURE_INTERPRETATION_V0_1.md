# Test 013C Architecture Interpretation V0.1

Test 013C is a replication of method, not coefficient transfer.

SPY discovered the architecture. QQQ and DIA each independently constructed their Price state and estimated local centers, scales, targets, and coefficients from their own causal histories. Across all three instruments, the same frozen state, affine ridge form, lambda, window authority, structural equations, and RK45 procedure produced the same broad stabilization signature relative to instrument-local F0.

This supports an adaptive Price-trajectory method rather than an SPY/QQQ-specific fitted phenomenon. It does not establish universal market dynamics: the instruments are related US ETFs, and transition/local-domain concerns remain.
