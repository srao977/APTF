## 3. Field reclassification

### 3.1 Direction

- **Previous:** GENUINE_D02_MATHEMATICAL_GAP.
- **Revised:** DETERMINISTIC_FMO_GEOMETRY.
- **Reasoning:** D02 can truthfully describe the terminal orientation of D01's own path without claiming future correctness.
- **Definition:** `path_direction = UPWARD` for positive terminal displacement, `DOWNWARD` for negative, and `FLAT` for exactly zero.
- **Human decision:** exact zero is final D02 semantics; no tolerance or materiality threshold is permitted in D02.
- **New science:** NO.

### 3.2 Shape quality

- **Previous:** GENUINE_D02_MATHEMATICAL_GAP.
- **Revised:** D04_LEGACY_NAME_MISMATCH / OBSOLETE META-SCORE.
- **Reasoning:** current D04 already receives separate magnitude, support, uncertainty, persistence, decay, and reversal dimensions. A further weighted quality score duplicates them. If regularity matters, frozen `coherence` and the full path are clearer inputs.
- **Human decision:** `shape_quality` remains removed. Any future distinct regularity quantity is a separately reviewed D04 or architecture proposal.
- **New science:** NO for D02.

### 3.3 Forward support

- **Previous:** GENUINE_D02_MATHEMATICAL_GAP.
- **Revised:** D04_LEGACY_NAME_MISMATCH.
- **Reasoning:** D01 already emits `state_support_ratio` and projected strength/persistence/uncertainty/reversal paths. D02 should expose them without inventing another support score.
- **Human decision:** D02 preserves natural unbounded `state_support_ratio` and full FMO support coordinates; D04 consumption design is deferred.
- **New science:** NO.

### 3.4 Expected lifetime

- **Previous:** GENUINE_D02_MATHEMATICAL_GAP.
- **Revised:** D04_LEGACY_NAME_MISMATCH.
- **Reasoning:** D01 directly defines `forward_interval`, the extent of the projected path. Calling it statistical expected lifetime overstates its meaning.
- **Human decision:** `projection_interval` retains projection-extent semantics; D04 owns newer-shape supersession and inclusive-endpoint staleness.
- **New science:** NO.

### 3.5 Magnitude score

- **Previous:** GENUINE_D02_MATHEMATICAL_GAP.
- **Revised:** DETERMINISTIC_FMO_GEOMETRY.
- **Reasoning:** terminal displacement and maximum absolute excursion are exact causal properties of the D01-generated path. No normalized attractiveness score belongs in D02.
- **Definition:** retain both signed terminal displacement and nonnegative maximum absolute displacement while preserving full samples.
- **Human decision:** D02 preserves terminal displacement, maximum absolute displacement, and full samples; any D04 scalarization/normalization is deferred to D04 design.
- **New science:** NO.

### 3.6 Decay score

- **Previous:** GENUINE_D02_MATHEMATICAL_GAP.
- **Revised:** D04_LEGACY_NAME_MISMATCH.
- **Reasoning:** D01 already defines $d_t(\tau)=2^{-\tau/H_{f,t}}$ and applies it to FMO coordinates.
- **Definition:** expose `forward_half_life`, full projected paths, and `terminal_decay_factor` at the projection endpoint.
- **Remaining definition:** D04 chooses whether terminal remaining influence or the complete decay path matters to capturability.
- **New science:** NO.

### 3.7 Reversal risk

- **Previous:** GENUINE_D02_MATHEMATICAL_GAP.
- **Revised:** D04_LEGACY_NAME_MISMATCH.
- **Reasoning:** the required coordinate already exists as `reversal_propensity`; renaming it risk suggests probability/decision authority not granted by D01.
- **Remaining definition:** none for D02; D04 may invert the propensity for its own transparent scoring.
- **New science:** NO.

### 3.8 Active

- **Previous:** GENUINE_D02_MATHEMATICAL_GAP.
- **Revised:** D04_LIFECYCLE_RESPONSIBILITY.
- **Reasoning:** active/expired status depends on evaluation time, supersession, and D04 lifecycle policy rather than new market inference.
- **Human decision:** newer same-entity shape supersedes immediately; valid through the inclusive endpoint; stale only afterward.
- **New science:** NO.

No one of the previous eight requires genuinely new market-state mathematics. Two are deterministic FMO geometry, five are legacy semantic/interface mismatches, and one is D04 lifecycle responsibility.
