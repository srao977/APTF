# SPY Cockpit Specification V0.1

Display SPY and the P Engine lamp, trajectory phase, confidence, domain state, numerical current/projected `[P,P1,P2]`, and reason codes. Mark V Engine as `NOT YET COMPLETE`, Execution Window and Position as `NOT YET ACTIVE`, Paper Account as `NOT YET ACTIVE`, and Broker as `NONE`.

GREEN/AMBER/RED describe Price trajectory state only. They are not trade actions. INVALID has no fabricated color and must be visibly distinct.
